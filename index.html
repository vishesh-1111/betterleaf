Questions engineers ask first.

Do I need to know LaTeX?

It helps, because you can edit the source directly. If you’d rather not, describe the change to the AI in plain English and it edits the LaTeX for you.

What can I upload?

A PDF or Word version of your current resume. It loads straight into the editor.

What does the free tier include?

The full editor, live preview, PDF download and a limited amount of AI usage. No card required.

Can I take my resume with me?

Yes. Download a PDF or the .tex file whenever you like.

What happens to my resume?

It’s yours. You can download or delete it at any time.
