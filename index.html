Questions engineers ask first.

Will it make up metrics?

No. Betterleaf only uses figures you give it. When a number is missing it asks, and you can leave it out. Anything you approximate is marked with a tilde so you remember to be ready to explain it.

Will my resume pass applicant tracking systems?

Templates use a single column, real selectable text and standard section headings, which is what parsers handle best. No tables, icons or text in images.

Does it work for roles outside backend and frontend?

Yes. ML, data, infrastructure, security, mobile and embedded each have their own vocabulary and kinds of impact, and the questions Betterleaf asks change to match.

What happens to my data?

Your resume is yours. You can export or delete it at any time. Add your privacy policy link here before launch.

I’m early in my career. Is this still useful?

Especially so. Internships, side projects and open-source work often have strong results that don’t get written down. Betterleaf helps you pull them out.
